Plan and execute multi-channel content distribution to maximize reach, engagement, and ROI.

## Channel Selection Criteria
- Evaluate each channel against: audience presence (are they there?), content format fit (native format strength), engagement norms (frequency, cadence, tone), and resource cost (production effort per piece).
- Prioritize 2-3 primary channels where the audience is most active and engaged - don't try to be everywhere.
- Assign channel roles: awareness (social/ads), nurture (email/RSS), authority (LinkedIn/long-form syndication).
- Re-evaluate channel mix quarterly based on performance data (CTR, engagement rate, attributed conversions).

## Optimal Posting Schedules
- **Email newsletters**: weekly or bi-weekly (not daily). Send on Tuesday-Thursday, mid-morning (10-11am recipient time).
- **LinkedIn**: 3-5x/week, native posts (not link shares). Best times: Tue-Thu, 8-10am and 12-1pm.
- **X/Twitter**: 1-3x/day. Peak engagement: 8-10am and 5-7pm weekdays.
- **Instagram**: 3-5x/week (feed), daily (Stories). Avoid weekends for B2B; weekends work for B2C.
- **RSS/feeds**: publish when new content goes live - no scheduling needed, but batch updates to avoid subscriber fatigue.

## Content Repurposing Per Channel
- **Long-form post** (blog, whitepaper) → LinkedIn carousel (key takeaways) + X thread (3-5 tweets expanding each point) + email summary with link.
- **Data/report** → infographic (Instagram/Pinterest) + quote cards (X/LinkedIn) + key stat in email subject line.
- **Video/podcast** → transcript (blog/SEO) + audiogram (social) + 3 pull quotes (X/LinkedIn) + "top 5 takeaways" email.
- **Case study** → customer quote card (social) + "how we did it" mini-blog + results screenshot (Instagram).

## Cross-Promotion Strategies
- Partner with complementary brands/creators for guest swaps, co-branded content, or shared giveaways.
- Cross-reference content across channels: "We covered this in depth in our newsletter - subscribe for the full breakdown."
- Use email to amplify social content and vice versa: embed top social posts in newsletter, promote newsletter signup in social bios.
- Create channel-exclusive content to give each audience a reason to follow on multiple platforms.
