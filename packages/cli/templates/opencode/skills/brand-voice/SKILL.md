Enforce the brand's voice across all copy and content by following these steps:

## Tone Analysis
- Analyze existing brand materials (website, social, ads) to extract tone attributes: formal/casual, serious/playful, technical/accessible.
- Classify tone on dimensions: formality, emotional range, jargon tolerance, sentence complexity, and cultural references.
- Document the brand's core personality traits (e.g., authoritative, friendly, innovative).

## Vocabulary Compliance
- Maintain a glossary of approved terms (what to say) and blocked terms (what to avoid).
- Flag any copy that uses disallowed vocabulary, brand-inconsistent synonyms, or overly generic phrasing.
- Suggest brand-aligned alternatives for flagged terms.

## Style Guide Enforcement
- Check all copy against the brand's mechanical rules: heading capitalization, punctuation style (Oxford comma yes/no), number formatting, date formats, link styling.
- Verify consistent use of brand names, trademarks, and product references (capitalization, spacing, symbols).
- Ensure acronyms are defined on first use unless the brand style guide specifies otherwise.

## Before/After Examples
- For any revision, provide a **before** (original text) and **after** (corrected version) to demonstrate the adjustment.
- Annotate the after version with inline comments noting which rule(s) each change enforces.
- Group examples by the dimension they address (tone, vocabulary, style).
