Apply SEO best practices to improve organic search visibility and content discoverability.

## Keyword Research and Placement
- Identify primary keyword (target search intent) and 2-3 secondary keywords per page.
- Place the primary keyword in: H1, first 100 words, one H2, image alt text, and the URL slug.
- Distribute secondary keywords naturally across H2s and body copy — never keyword-stuff.
- Use semantic/LSI terms (topically related words) to strengthen relevance signals.

## Title Tag and Meta Description Formulas
- **Title tag**: Primary keyword + secondary keyword (or brand) | 50-60 characters. Front-load the keyword.
- **Meta description**: 150-160 characters. Include primary keyword, a benefit statement, and a CTA. Entice the click without misleading.
- Every page must have a unique title tag and meta description — no duplicates.

## Heading Hierarchy
- One H1 per page, matching the user's search intent and containing the primary keyword.
- H2s represent major sections; include one H2 with the primary keyword variant.
- H3+ for subsections — use only when there are at least two subordinate points under a given H2.
- Headings must be descriptive of the content below, not generic ("Introduction", "More").

## Internal Linking
- Each page should link to 3-5 other relevant pages on the site.
- Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text — never "click here" or "read more".
- Link from high-authority pages to deeper content to pass link equity.
- Check for and fix orphan pages (pages with zero internal links pointing to them).

## Readability Scoring
- Target grade level 7-9 for general audiences; grade 10+ for technical/B2B content.
- Keep sentences under 20 words on average. Break up paragraphs to 3-4 lines max.
- Use bullet points, bold text, and short subheadings to improve scannability.
- Test with a readability tool (e.g., Hemingway, Flesch-Kincaid) and adjust until score is within range.
